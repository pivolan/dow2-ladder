Elite Lab

Experiments

Add-ons and tools we are testing with the community. Easiest way: install the launcher and tick them in “Add-ons” — they update themselves. Or download each one below.

These are prototypes made by a community member, not by the Elite team. If the game crashes, press “Send logs” in the launcher before starting the game again.

In-game add-ons

LobbyStats v0.7.0 test

Relic rating next to every name in the lobby, a tooltip with win rate, games and Steam hours. Only shows information, never kicks anyone.

  • Rating next to the name: pivolan [2061]
  • Hover a player: rating, win rate, wins/losses, games, Steam hours
Download .zip Stats on the web

FrameProbe v9 diagnostics

Developer logs: what slows the game down when you spectate, and what the game sees when you press Continue after a match. Doesn't change the game.

  • Observer: FPS, frame times, simulation ticks every 10 seconds
  • Continue: state of the score screen at the moment of the click
  • v8: no crashes at start or on Continue after a replay
Download .zip

ObsFix v1 test

Fix for observer lag: while spectating a live game the game rebuilt the whole match info dozens of times per frame just to find its own slot. ObsFix remembers the answer.

  • Live spectating only; players and replays are not affected
  • Off by default in the launcher — tick it, spectate, send logs
Download .zip

Install an add-on by hand

  1. Put the .dll from the zip into <Steam>\steamapps\common\Dawn of War II - Retribution\Elite_DLLs\
  2. Open Elite.launchconfig in the same game folder with Notepad and add , LobbyStats.dll (or , FrameProbe.dll) to the end of the InjectedFiles= line.
  3. Start Elite as usual. The add-on writes its log next to the dll; the first line shows its version.
